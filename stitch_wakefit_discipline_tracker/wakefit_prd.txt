# WakeFit Discipline Tracker - Product Requirements Document

## Overview
WakeFit is a personal discipline and health tracking app designed for iOS. The tone is motivational, clean, and focused—like a strict personal coach in your pocket.

## Target Device
iPhone 15 Pro to iPhone 17 Pro Max (Portrait)

## Visual Identity
- **Mode:** Dark Mode (Primary)
- **Base Background:** #0D0F1A
- **Surface/Cards:** #1C1F2E
- **Primary Accent:** #00D4B4 (Electric Teal)
- **Warning:** #F5A623 (Amber)
- **Danger:** #FF5C5C (Coral Red)
- **Success:** #00C896 (Teal Green)
- **Typography:** SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text
- **Border Radius:** 16px

## Screen List
1. **Login Screen:** Minimal, "Your discipline starts here", Google Sign-in.
2. **Profile Setup:** Name, weight (start/target), height.
3. **Dashboard (Home):** Discipline score ring, Wake-up log, Food summary, Calories, Weight trend, Streak.
4. **Wake-Up Log:** Time picker wheel, history list.
5. **Food Log:** 3 time blocks (Morning, Afternoon, Evening), expandable cards.
6. **Add Food Item:** Bottom sheet, voice/microphone input, transcription.
7. **Sleep Log:** "Did you sleep on time?", binary choice, notes, 7-day history.
8. **AI Analysis:** Calories, Protein, Discipline status, Coach Diagnosis, Advice.
9. **Weight Tracking:** Stats, line graph with target line, log button.
10. **Notification Overlay:** Calorie warning, motivational quote.
11. **Search:** Segmented controls, list results.
12. **Profile/Settings:** User stats, toggles, sign out.

## Key Components
- Discipline score ring (Circular progress)
- Food time block cards
- Stat cards
- Bottom sheet modal
- Streak counter (Flame)
